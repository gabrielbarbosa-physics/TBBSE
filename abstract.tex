\begin{center}
    \textbf{Abstract}
\end{center}

In this first-principles study, we perform calculations on 2D/3D heterostructures consisting of Transition Metal Dichalcogenide (TMD) monolayers vertically stacked on oriented GaAs substrates. We calculate the optical properties of these materials by solving the Bethe-Salpeter equation via a tight-binding approach, constructed from maximally localized Wannier functions derived from HSE06 hybrid functional calculations.
